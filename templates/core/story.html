Our Story

The Gerardo's Story

Gerardo's Italian Bakery was founded in 1993 by Gerardo Sarli.

Gerardo learned his craft as an apprentice under traditional master bakers, mastering the techniques behind Italian breads, pastries and wedding cakes long before opening his own shop. Encouraged by his mother, he eventually set out to build a bakery of his own — one rooted in the family recipes carried over from Italy.

More than thirty years later, that same philosophy still shapes every loaf, pastry and cake that leaves our ovens: quality ingredients, traditional technique, and recipes passed down through family.
